SYS 3062 Lab Project Assumptions
Sebastian Nowicki & Pearce Buckley

Assumptions:
1. The system operates from 4 am to 8 pm, every day, 7 days a week.
    Those still in the system at 8pm will be serviced until 8:30, at which point they will be turned away (passengers in service @ 8:30 will have service completed)
    Arrivals will stop at 8 pm
2. Each passenger has a check bag with p = .45 (sourced from TSA data)
    Modelled as a bernouli(.45) for each passenger
3. Customers with a check bag must be routed to bag check
    before security
4. There are two bag check servers, customers will route to
    whichever one is open (shared queue) - if both are open they will choose server 1
5. There are two security checkpoints (shared cue).
6. The second security checkpoint opens when the queue is longer than 10, and will shut down when the queue goes below 3, after finishing service of current customer
7. All queues are assummed to have limitless capacity
8. There is no balking, as all passengers need to make their flights
9. Inter-arival times (minutes) are dictated by a Burr 4p (2.08, 1.76, 5.37, -.05) RV (All negative values will be clipped to zero)
10. Bag Check service times (seconds) are dictated by a lognormal (4.64, .29) RV
11. Security checkpoint service times (seconds) are dictated by a Generalized Gamma (24.72, 3.77, 3.02) RV
    * All RV's based on phitter analysis of generated data
12. One run of the simulation will be considered running the simulation for one day

Goal: Estimate waiting times at bag check and security over an operating day, and see what changes them.

Measures: Mean queue wait at bag check and at security, and mean time in system.
    Utilization (bag check, lane 1, lane 2), max queue lengths, fraction of the day lane 2 is open, passengers turned away at 8:30.

Runs: 20 replications.

Out of scope: No PreCheck lane, no secondary screening, no check-in step, no airline-specific counters, no staff breaks, no flight delays.

Data: Input data are synthetic (simulated by MuseAI, based on iFly wait/flight times).

Validation: Compare mean security wait to the iFly wait times.

Parameters: Burr 4p (2.08, 1.76, 5.37, -.05) = (scale, c, d, loc).
    Lognormal (4.64, .29) = (log-mean, log-sd).
    Generalized Gamma (24.72, 3.77, 3.02) = (scale, d, p); in scipy use gengamma(a=1.248, c=3.02, scale=24.72).

Notes: "The queue" in 6 is the waiting queue (passengers waiting, not in service).
    Passengers who finish bag check after 8:30 are turned away (no new service starts after 8:30).
